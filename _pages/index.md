# SPIN 2027

<p style="text-align: center;font-size:21px">
33rd International Symposium on Model Checking Software
</p>

<p style="text-align: center;font-size:18px">
Co-located with <a href="https://etaps.org/2027/cfp/">ETAPS 2027</a> in Copenhagen, Denmark.<br>April 14–15, 2027.
</p>

<p>
The 33rd International SPIN symposium on Model Checking of Software (SPIN 2027) will be held in Copenhagen, Denmark, on 14 and 15 April 2027. SPIN 2027 is the latest in a successful series of workshops and symposia for practitioners and researchers interested in symbolic and state space-based techniques for the validation and analysis of software systems. Techniques and empirical evaluations based on explicit representations of state spaces, as implemented in the SPIN model checker or other tools, or techniques based on the combination of explicit representations with other representations, are the focus of this symposium.
</p>

<p>
SPIN 2027 will be co-located with the 30th European Joint Conferences on Theory and Practice of Software (<a href="https://etaps.org/2027/cfp/">ETAPS 2027</a>).
</p>
